Delete your Expense Manager account

Expense Manager (iOS and Android) · published by TECH LOGIC HOST

An account is only needed for Shared spaces. Your personal transactions are stored on your phone and are never uploaded to our server.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Expense Manager and go to Shared spaces.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Delete without the app

Send an email to contact@appspedia.net from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Expense Manager account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address and account ID.
  • Your sign-in sessions and device tokens.
  • Your memberships in shared spaces, and spaces you own that have no other members.

What is kept

  • Expenses you added to shared spaces that other members still use stay visible to them, without your name or email, so their balances remain correct.
  • Short-lived server logs (kept for at most 30 days for security), then removed.

Data on your phone

Personal data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. An Android backup can be removed in Android Settings → Google → Backup; an iCloud backup in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

See also our Privacy Policy.
