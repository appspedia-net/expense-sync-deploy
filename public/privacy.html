Privacy Policy

Expense Manager (iOS and Android) · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Expense Manager ("the app") is published by TECH LOGIC HOST. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your personal finances stay on your device, we never sell your data, and the app has no third-party advertising or tracking.

Data that stays on your device

Your transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, debts, bills, categories, receipts and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. We cannot see this data.

iCloud backup (optional). If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy of your data is saved in your own private iCloud Drive space for this app. It is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Google backup (Android, optional). If backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android saves a copy of the app's data in your Google account. It is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. You can also save a full backup file yourself and keep it wherever you like.

App lock. Fingerprint, face and Face ID / Touch ID unlock use your device's own biometric system. The app never receives or stores biometric data.

Shared spaces (optional, needs an account)

Shared spaces let you track and split expenses with family, friends or a group. Only if you use this feature, you sign in and we store the following on our sync server (sync.imrashid.net):

  • Account details: your name (if you provide it), your email address, and an account ID. You can sign in with Apple (Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one) or with a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Shared-space content: the spaces you create or join, their members, and the expenses, splits and settlements added to those spaces, so every member sees the same data.
  • Invite links you create, and basic technical data needed to run the service securely (for example IP address in short-lived server logs and rate limits).

Your personal (non-shared) transactions are never uploaded to our server.

AI features (optional)

If you use receipt scanning or ask a question to the AI assistant, the receipt image or your question (with the summary needed to answer it) is sent through our server to OpenAI for processing, and the result is returned to the app. We do not keep the image or the question after the request is completed, apart from a daily usage counter.

App configuration and promotions

When the app starts it downloads a small settings file from imrashid.net (for example to show an update notice). The request includes your device's country code so the right settings can be returned; it contains no personal information. The app may show promotions for our own other apps. No advertising networks or tracking tools are included in the app.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details.

How we use and share data

  • We use your data to provide the app's features (sign-in, syncing shared spaces, login codes, AI results), and we may use your email address to send you news about Expense Manager and our other apps. Every such email has an unsubscribe link.
  • We do not sell your data, do not share it with advertisers or ad networks, and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Service providers that help run the features: our hosting provider (server and email for login codes), Apple (Sign in with Apple, iCloud, App Store purchases), Google (Google Play purchases, Android backup) and OpenAI (only when you use AI features).
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Shared-space data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Shared spaces → account icon at the top → Delete account). This removes your account details (name, profile, sign-in links) and your memberships; expenses you added to spaces that other members still use remain visible to them without your name or email. Your email address is kept after deletion, stored separately with the dates your account was created and deleted, and may be used to contact you about Expense Manager and our other apps (each email has an unsubscribe link). To have your email erased completely, write to contact@appspedia.net. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app; your iCloud backup can be deleted from iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud, and your Android backup from Android Settings → Google → Backup. You can also request account deletion without the app: see Delete your account.

Security

All communication with our server uses HTTPS. Access to shared spaces is limited to their members.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.

Contact
